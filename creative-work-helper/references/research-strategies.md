# Research Strategies

Start with a decision-relevant research question, such as: “Which participation mechanisms produce useful contributions without requiring high audience effort?”

## Search lanes

- Direct: comparable category, audience, channel, or objective.
- Adjacent: a different field solving the same behavior or communication problem.
- Mechanism: interaction, narrative, visual system, distribution, or participation pattern.
- Constraint: examples succeeding under similar budget, assets, time, or governance.
- Counterexample: saturated patterns, failures, backlash, or conditions where a mechanism breaks.

## Reference card

For each useful case capture:

- the actual mechanism—not its surface look;
- why it worked and for whom;
- enabling conditions and resources;
- transferable components;
- mismatch, imitation, cultural, rights, and platform risks;
- the creative decision it informs.

Stop when new cases repeat known mechanisms or evidence is sufficient to choose the next experiment. A second round should answer a sharper question created by feedback.
