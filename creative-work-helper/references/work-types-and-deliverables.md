# Work Types and Deliverables

Use these as starting hypotheses, never fixed rankings or mandatory templates.

## Content or social series

Likely priorities: audience relevance, platform fit, repeatability, production sustainability, recognizability. General package: editorial premise, audience promise, pillars, recurring formats, sample posts/episodes, cadence, system rules, production workflow, measures and risks.

## Campaign or communication concept

Likely priorities: goal fit, central idea strength, audience comprehension, channel extensibility, feasibility and risk. General package: problem, insight, proposition, concept, audience journey, channel roles, hero and supporting executions, production needs, measures, assumptions.

## Event or experience

Likely priorities: participant value, journey coherence, operational feasibility, safety/accessibility, capacity and cost. General package: experience promise, audience journey, program/interaction modules, space/channel logic, staffing, operations, contingency, measures.

## Visual or content system

Likely priorities: recognition, flexibility, content–form fit, maintainability, accessibility. General package: principle, invariants, variables, boundaries, components, example applications, governance and QA.

## Single creative execution

Likely priorities: communication clarity, emotional effect, craft, context/platform fit, production feasibility. General package: concept, key message, narrative/layout, style, specifications, asset list, production steps, review checklist.

## Asset curation or grouping

Likely priorities: evidence fit, meaningful clusters, coverage, asset quality, narrative usefulness, honest labels. General package: inventory, selection logic, clusters, overlaps, exclusions, gaps, proposed sequence or system, sourcing needs.

## Product, service, or interactive concept

Likely priorities: user value, behavioral fit, usability, technical feasibility, differentiation and validation cost. General package: user problem, insight, value proposition, interaction loop, journey, key features, prototype scope, assumptions, tests and risks.

## Selecting priorities and output

Infer the work type, project stage, goal, constraints, and decision context. Propose priorities as Tentative, explain their order, and invite overrides. Ask what the user must deliver. If unclear, offer the relevant general package above and let the user remove, reorder, or extend components.
